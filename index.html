IS THIS A MOTORCYCLE? IS A MOTORCYCLE LICENSE REQUIRED?

No, the Polaris Slingshot is classified as an autocycle in the State of Florida and only a valid driver's license is required. You can use your regular car driver license and be ready for the experience.

ARE ANY SPECIAL SKILLS REQUIRED TO DRIVE A SLINGSHOT?

No, just a valid regular driver license is required. You can use your foreign driver license. If you know how to drive a car you'll be able to experience the Polaris Slingshot.

WHAT ARE THE AGE REQUIREMENTS FOR DRIVERS AND PASSENGERS IN A SLINGSHOT?

Renters and all drivers must be a minimum of 21 years of age. Any passengers must be able to sit firmly in the seat with their feet flat on the floor and properly restrained by the seatbelt. Children requiring use of a car seat are not permitted.

WHAT ABOUT SLINGSHOT INSURANCE

Neither your auto insurance, nor your credit card, covers vehicles with less than 4 wheels. Liability insurance coverage is provided through our booking platforms and is included with your rental.

HOW FAR AND WHERE CAN I GO IN A SLINGSHOT?

Slingshots are a licensed, registered, street legal vehicle, that may go anywhere any other road vehicle may go, subject to the same governing rules, laws and regulations. Every rental is with unlimited miles to drive anywhere in Miami-Dade and Broward.

WHAT IS THE REFUELING POLICY FOR A SLINGSHOT RENTAL?

Renter is responsible to refuel the Slingshot to the same fuel level as when received. Minimum octane 93. Because it's a high compression engine it requires specific high octane gas.

WHAT IS THE CANCELLATION POLICY FOR A SLINGSHOT RENTAL?
