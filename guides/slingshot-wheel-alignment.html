What you’ll learn

5 quick, illustrated steps
1What “toe” is and why the Slingshot needs slight toe-out
2How to chalk-mark and measure toe correctly (the part most people get wrong)
3How to adjust toe by turning the tie rods
4How to center a crooked steering wheel without changing the toe
5Why you must recalibrate the steering angle sensor (SAS) afterward

Steps 1–4 you can do yourself. Step 5 (SAS) needs the dealer’s Digital Wrench scan tool.
