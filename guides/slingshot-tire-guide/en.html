Slingshot Tire Guide: Pressure, Wear & Sizes

One wide rear tire takes 100% of the power — which makes Slingshot tires wear differently than any car. Pressures, sizes, wear-pattern diagnosis and replacement rules.

Pressures & sizes

check these cold, monthly
🛞Pressure (cold): 28 psi front / 32 psi rear — low pressure is the #1 cause of vague steering
📏Sizes vary by year/trim: commonly 225/45R18 up front with an 18″ or 20″ wide rear (e.g. 305-series on R models). Confirm on your door placard or manual before buying
⚠Rear is special: it’s a wide automotive tire taking 100% of the power — quality matters more here than anywhere
2

Read your wear pattern

tap what you see

Worn down the middle = overinflation. Drop to spec (28 front / 32 rear, cold) and the contact patch spreads back out.

Worn on both edges = underinflation. Low pressure also makes steering heavy and heats the tire. Air up to spec and re-check weekly.

One shoulder worn = alignment. On the fronts this is toe/camber — see our Wheel Alignment guide. On the rear, check belt tracking too.

Cupping / scalloped patches = balance or worn suspension components. Get the wheel balanced and inspect shocks and bearings.

Rear tire going bald quickly is normal-ish: one wide tire takes all the power. Spirited driving can eat a rear in 8,000–15,000 miles. Smooth throttle = more miles.

Replacement rules

when tread talks, listen
1Replace at 2/32″ tread (penny test) — earlier if you ride in rain (see the Rain guide)
2No rotation for the rear (there’s only one) — fronts can swap side-to-side if non-directional
3Uneven front wear? Fix the alignment first or the new tires die the same death
4Age counts: 6+ year-old rubber hardens and loses wet grip even with tread left

Tire Guide — FAQ

What tire pressure does a Polaris Slingshot use?

28 psi front and 32 psi rear, measured cold. Check monthly — underinflation causes vague steering, edge wear and heat.

What size tires does a Slingshot have?

It varies by year and trim: commonly 225/45R18 up front, with a wide 18- or 20-inch rear (305-series on R models). Always confirm your exact size on the door placard or owner's manual.

How long does a Slingshot rear tire last?

Typically 8,000–15,000 miles — the single wide rear takes all the power, so throttle-happy driving shortens its life dramatically.

Why is my Slingshot rear tire wearing so fast?

One drive wheel handles all the torque and traction-control interventions. It's normal for the rear to wear 2–3× faster than the fronts; smooth throttle extends it.

Can I rotate tires on a Slingshot?

There's no front-to-rear rotation (different sizes and only one rear). Non-directional front tires can be swapped side to side to even out wear.
