Short answer: premium only — 91 octane minimum, 93 recommended. Here is why the engine demands it, what regular gas actually does, and the numbers on tank, mpg and range.

What happens if you use regular?

knock, retard, lost power

Low octane fuel ignites too early under high compression — that's engine knock. The ECU protects itself by retarding ignition timing, which means less power, worse mpg and long-term risk of piston damage if you keep doing it. One accidental tank of 89 won't destroy the engine — just drive gently and fill with 93 as soon as possible. A habit of regular gas will.

Slingshot Gas — FAQ

What kind of gas does a Polaris Slingshot take?

Premium unleaded — 91 octane minimum per Polaris, and 93 octane is what we recommend and run in our Miami rental fleet. Never regular 87 and never E85.

What happens if I put regular gas in a Slingshot?

The high-compression engine will knock, and the ECU will pull ignition timing — you lose power and mpg. One tank won't ruin it if you drive gently and refill with premium, but repeated regular gas risks engine damage.

How big is the Slingshot gas tank?

9.77 gallons (37 liters), good for roughly 250–280 miles at the typical 23–33 mpg owners see.

Can a Slingshot use E85 or ethanol fuel?

No E85. Standard pump premium with up to 10% ethanol (E10) is approved; higher ethanol blends are not.

What octane do rental Slingshots in Miami need at return?

93 octane — return the Slingshot at the same fuel level it went out with, filled with premium. Any major-brand 93 pump is fine.
