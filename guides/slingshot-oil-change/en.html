The change, step by step

15–20 minutes with basic tools
1Warm the engine 2–3 minutes, park on level ground, engine off
2Remove the drain plug, drain fully, install plug with a new crush washer

Oil Change — FAQ

What oil does a Polaris Slingshot take?

2020+ models with the ProStar 2.0 engine take full synthetic 5W-50 (Polaris PS-4 or equivalent), about 6 quarts with filter. 2015–2019 models with the GM Ecotec 2.4L take synthetic 5W-30 per the owner's manual.

How often should I change the oil on a Slingshot?

On 2020+ models, every 5,000–7,500 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first. On 2015–2019 models, roughly every 6,000–7,500 miles or annually. Ride hard or in heat? Lean toward the shorter interval.

Can I change Slingshot oil myself?

Yes — it's a 15–20 minute job with basic tools: drain, new crush washer, new filter, fill to spec and re-check the level. Dispose of used oil at any auto parts store.

What happens if I skip oil changes?

The ProStar is a high-revving engine — old oil accelerates wear, can trigger low-oil-pressure issues and voids goodwill on warranty claims. It's the cheapest insurance the vehicle has.

Does the Slingshot need any other regular fluids?

Yes: angle drive fluid (80W-250, change at 50,000 miles), brake fluid and coolant inspections per the schedule, plus belt tension checks — see our other guides for each.
