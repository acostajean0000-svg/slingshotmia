Best Slingshot Routes in Miami

A Slingshot turns an ordinary drive into the main event. These are the five routes we send our renters on — from the 1-hour Ocean Drive classic to a full day in the Keys — with local tips on timing, parking and photos.

Five routes worth the drive

tap one to see the details

~1 hour · the classic. Cross the MacArthur Causeway into South Beach with the skyline in your mirrors, then cruise Ocean Drive past the Art Deco hotels — slow, loud and full of people photographing you. Loop back on Collins Ave and stop at Lummus Park for pictures.

Best at golden hour. Parking is tight — the Slingshot fits where cars don’t.

~2 hours · the fast, open one. Take the Rickenbacker Causeway — the bridge climb with Biscayne Bay on both sides is the single best stretch of road in the city. Continue to Crandon Park and Bill Baggs / Cape Florida lighthouse at the tip. Open water views the whole way.

Small toll on the causeway. Windy, fast and spectacular.

~3–4 hours · the coast run. Head north on A1A through Sunny Isles, Hollywood and Hallandale to Fort Lauderdale beach. Ocean on your right almost the entire way, dozens of places to stop. Come back on US-1 for variety.

Great with a 4-hour or full-day rental — unlimited miles and SunPass included.

Full day · the bucket-list one. South on US-1 through Key Largo, Islamorada and the Seven Mile Bridge — water on both sides, horizon everywhere. Key West is ~3.5 hours each way, so this is a 24-hour rental trip, not a half-day.

Fuel range is roughly 250–280 miles (see specs) — fill up before the long bridges.

~1–2 hours · the light show. Downtown and Brickell after dark, across the bridges with the skyline lit up, then Wynwood for the murals and street life. The Slingshot’s lighting makes it a rolling attraction at night.

We rent until 11:45 PM — night rides are underrated and cooler in summer.

📸Best photo spots: Lummus Park (Art Deco backdrop), the Rickenbacker bridge crest, Wynwood walls, Cape Florida lighthouse

Miami Scenic Routes — FAQ

What's the best Slingshot route in Miami?

The classic is the MacArthur Causeway into South Beach and down Ocean Drive at golden hour. For open-road driving, the Rickenbacker Causeway to Key Biscayne is the best stretch in the city — water on both sides the whole way.

Can you drive a Slingshot to the Florida Keys?

Yes. US-1 south through Key Largo, Islamorada and the Seven Mile Bridge is spectacular. Key West is about 3.5 hours each way, so plan a 24-hour rental rather than a half-day.

Can a Slingshot go on the highway in Miami?

Yes — it's a street-legal autocycle, so I-95, the causeways, the turnpike and A1A are all fine. Our rentals include SunPass for tolls and unlimited miles.

How long should I rent a Slingshot for in Miami?

One hour covers a South Beach loop; two hours is the sweet spot for Ocean Drive plus Key Biscayne; four hours reaches Fort Lauderdale; and 24 hours opens up the Keys.

What's the best time of day to drive a Slingshot in Miami?

Golden hour — roughly an hour before sunset — for cooler temperatures, the best light and peak Ocean Drive atmosphere. Night rides through Brickell and Wynwood are a close second.
