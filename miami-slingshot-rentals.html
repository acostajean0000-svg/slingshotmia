Slingshot Rental Questions and Answers

IS THIS A MOTORCYCLE? IS A MOTORCYCLE LICENSE REQUIRED?

No, the Polaris Slingshot is classified as an autocycle in the State of Florida and only a valid drivers license is required.

ARE ANY SPECIAL SKILLS REQUIRED TO DRIVE A SLINGSHOT?

Yes, a valid drivers license is required.

WHAT ARE THE AGE REQUIREMENTS FOR DRIVERS AND PASSENGERS?

Renter and all drivers must be a minimum of 21 years of age. Any passengers must be able to sit firmly in the seat with their feet flat on the floor and properly restrained by the seatbelt. Children requiring use of a car seat are not permitted.

WHAT ABOUT INSURANCE

Neither your auto insurance, nor your credit card, covers vehicles with less than 4 wheels. Liability insurance coverage is provided through our booking platforms and is included with your rental.

HOW FAR AND WHERE CAN I GO?

Slingshots are a licensed, registered, street legal vehicle, that may go anywhere any other road vehicle may go, subject to the same governing rules, laws and regulations. Every rental includes 300 miles per day. Additional mileage is charged at $0.49 per mile.

WHAT IS THE REFUELING POLICY?

Renter is responsible to refuel the Slingshot to the same fuel level as when received. Minimum octane 93.

WHAT IS THE CANCELLATION POLICY?

Please see the cancellation policy provided by the booking platform of your choice.

IS THERE A SECURITY DEPOSIT?

Of course there is! A security deposit will be billed separately from your rental at the time of booking. The security deposit amount may vary based upon driving record or previous rental experience. Deposit releases, returns, refunds or charges due are processed upon rental return. Renter is responsible for any damages above and beyond security deposit amounts.

RENTAL COST SLINGSHOT IN MIAMI?

Across most rental companies in Florida, it costs around $100-300 for a 1-day Polaris Slingshot rental. The vast majority of rental companies offer deals for multi-day rentals.
