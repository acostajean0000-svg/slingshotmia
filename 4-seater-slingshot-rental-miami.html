4 Seater Slingshot Rental — FAQ

How much does a 4 seater Slingshot rental cost in Miami?

Our 4-seater Polaris Slingshot rents for $180 for 2 hours, $280 for 4 hours, $320 for 6 hours or $400 for 8 hours — unlimited miles, SunPass for tolls and delivery in Miami-Dade & Broward included.

Do I need a special license to drive a 4 seater Slingshot?

No — like the 2-seater, the 4-seater Slingshot is an autocycle in Florida. A regular driver's license is all you need (foreign licenses welcome). Drivers must be 21+.

How many people fit in a 4 seater Slingshot?

Four adults — two in front, two in back, each with a full seat and seatbelt. It's the perfect middle option between our 2-seater and 6-seater Slingshots.

Do you deliver the 4 seater Slingshot?

Yes — delivery to your hotel or address anywhere in Miami-Dade and Broward County is included, from South Beach to Fort Lauderdale.

What's the difference between the 4 seater and the 6 seater?

The 4-seater is nimbler and cheaper — ideal for two couples or a small family. The 6-seater fits larger groups. Both are automatic and drive with a regular license.
