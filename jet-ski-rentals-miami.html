Jet Ski Rental FAQs

How far is the jet ski rental from South Beach?

About 15–20 minutes by car up Collins Avenue (A1A) from Miami Beach — Haulover Park is the closest full-service jet ski launch to South Beach, with free parking at the marina.

Do I need a license to rent a jet ski in Miami?

Riders born after January 1, 1988 must complete a temporary boating safety test.

Where are your jet ski rentals located?

Our jet ski rentals operate from Haulover Park at the Roz and Cal Kovens Conference Center near Sunny Isles Beach in Miami.

How long can I rent a jet ski?

We offer 1 hour, 2 hour, and 3 hour jet ski rental options depending on how long you want to enjoy the ride.

Is jet skiing safe for beginners?

Yes. Our staff provides safety instructions before every ride, and life jackets are included with every rental.

Can two people ride one jet ski?

Yes. Most jet skis allow two riders, making it a great activity for couples or friends.

What areas can we ride the jet skis?

Riders enjoy the beautiful waters near Sunny Isles Beach, Haulover Park, and Biscayne Bay.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended, especially during weekends and holidays, to guarantee availability.
